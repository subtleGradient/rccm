# 2026-09-14 — OpenFOAM hello world in ParaView

The user installed OpenFOAM and ParaView and requested a simple working
example before considering visualization of RCCM's asymmetric metric tensor.

Created `examples/openfoam-cavity/`: a 10 cm sliding-lid cavity, 1 m/s lid,
kinematic viscosity 0.01 m²/s, Reynolds number 10, 40 × 40 × 1 mesh,
2-second simulation, 0.0025-second step, and 81 saved times including zero.
Adapted the installed OpenCFD v2512 `icoFoam` tutorial and retained its
headers and GPL licence. This executable distribution is distinct from the
Foundation development source checkout linked as `OpenFOAM-dev`.

Runtime: `~/bin/openfoam`, mounted at `/Volumes/OpenFOAM-v2512`, native
`darwin64ClangDPInt32Opt`, build `_87ed40d256-20251219`. ParaView 5.13.3 at
`~/Applications/ParaView.app` supplies both the GUI and `pvpython`.
No Docker service or Foundation source build was needed.

Verification completed:

- `blockMesh` and `checkMesh`: 1,600 cells, `Mesh OK.`
