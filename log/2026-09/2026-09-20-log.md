## Lesson 1 — Find a slot

- User approved producing the first lesson. Added the shared `Axis` / `Slot`
  implementation and `row`, `column`, `swap` operations under
  `bend/asymmetric-metric-tensor/`.
- `LAWS.bend` states the agreed row exchange, column exchange and double-swap
  contracts. `PROOF.bend` imports them and proves all three for arbitrary
  slots by constructor cases and reflexivity. No new arithmetic is needed.
- `lessons/01-slots.md` keeps the full source component map visible, separates
  one physical sample from its sixteen component addresses, and connects a
  prediction, direct Bend run, types, notation, contract and proof.
- The runnable demonstration prints every address and `tx -> xt -> tx`.
- The no-op teaching module has a valid proof of double application being
  identity. Its separate counterexample is intentionally rejected because
  row `t` does not equal the required row `x`; it is not imported by the
  successful package gate.
