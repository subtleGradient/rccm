## Durable findings

- Display resolution is an observation choice, independent of physical geometry.
- A coarse tensor requires mean(1/q), not just 1/mean(q).
- Zero signed mean can coexist with nonzero microscopic/RMS activity.
- One tensor sample cannot recover topology; a toroidal reference overlay has independent geometry provenance.
- Static pressure, stress deviation, face traction, resultant and motion need separate readings.
- The current focused TeX includes an explicit §16.2 interaction atlas; its Coulomb/positronium entry describes aligned slip between counter-rotating defects.
