Renderer contract

Each mark has one job

Scalar → amount

The amber channel encodes q or its reciprocal, with the mapping stated beside the view. q is remaining static pressure relative to Pc. The pressure field has a value at a point; force needs variation across neighboring points.

Vector → arrow

The cyan channel is αv⊥/c. Its length carries magnitude and its head carries direction. Here only x is active. Reversing the x direction exchanges the signs of Û0x and Ûx0.

Oriented plane → loop

The rose channel is αtₚΩ. Its z axis names the x–y rotation plane. Arrowheads carry its handedness; the paired entries are Ûxy and Ûyx. This local loop is a glyph for vorticity. A particle’s tornadonut shape emerges from a whole field of samples.

Inside this fixture

A pressure budget you can audit

Set α = 1, v∥ = 0, and the separate structural load Pshear = 0. Use a rigid-rotation sample of radius r = 2ctₚ. Since Ω = 2ω, its edge speed satisfies v↻/c = tₚΩ = w. Rotation’s kinetic load remains in Pdyn. Section 2’s normalized kinetic ledger then gives this page’s exact control rule:

q = 1 − L − s² − w²

The control ranges keep q ≥ 0.1775. No clipping silently removes load. Changing the renderer preserves every matrix entry. The cage’s line count is a quantized display of 1/q; the numeric instrument retains the full value.

One move to make

Reverse first. Then amplify.

At the initial state, flip slip from +0.35 to −0.35. Predict which matrix cells and amber features will change. Then drag its magnitude to 0.50.

Reveal the invariant

The sign flip changes only Û0x and Ûx0, plus the cyan direction. q stays 0.615 and 1/q stays about 1.626. At |s| = 0.50 with the other initial controls fixed, q falls to 0.4875 and 1/q rises to about 2.051. Direction changed the shear; magnitude changed the shared budget.
