# Hyperslice: RCCM Escape Routes from a Superconductivity No-Go

## Hyperblob

The target is not “things that sound superconducting.” It is the full space of
RCCM-compatible current-carrying material states at and above room temperature,
including states that dissipate, states that do not, false-positive
measurements, and states RCCM cannot presently classify.

Each razor asks whether a proposed tau-fluidic invalidation actually covers
both halves. If either half remains open, the universal no-go fails.

| **Topological protection** | Relaxing/open trajectories | Closed/pinned/persistent circulation | RCCM itself places persistent objects in Blob B | The corpus explicitly admits protected zero-kinematic-resistance circulation. |

## Two-razor squares

### Geometry × texture

| | \(\nabla^2\mathbf v\ne0\) | \(\nabla^2\mathbf v=0\) |
|---|---|---|
| **Longitudinal** | Ordinary dissipative transport is possible | A lossless longitudinal route remains mathematically open |
| **Transverse/topological** | Moving vortices can dissipate | Pinned/quantized response can persist without longitudinal loss |

The proposed \(h/q^2\) and \(\mu>0\) no-go arguments each occupy only part of
this square.

### Evidence × scope

| | LK-99 | Any room-temperature material |
|---|---|---|
| **Observed failure** | Filled: independent studies reject superconductivity | Empty: no universal experiment exists |
| **Observed success** | Empty under current independent evidence | Empty at ambient pressure near 300 K; high-pressure hydrides approach it |

The empty bottom-right cell is an experimental unknown, not a proof of
impossibility.

## Value inversion

- **Finite loss is useful:** it supplies a possible decay mechanism and a
  measurable prediction if RCCM derives its coupling.
- **Lossless flow is dangerous:** without a formation/stability law it can
  become an unfalsifiable escape hatch.
- **A failed viral claim is useful:** it reveals the exact impurity and
  measurement controls a future claim must survive.
- **An open possibility is not encouraging evidence:** “not ruled out” remains
  many rungs below “predicted” or “observed.”

## Unsliceable boundary

The slicing stops at a missing orthogonal space: **material microphysics**.
RCCM currently does not map composition, lattice, carrier interactions,
temperature, and disorder into a superconducting order/stability equation.
More conceptual slicing cannot substitute for that forward model.

## Robustness ranking

1. **Strongest:** claim scope, response geometry, model status, evidence status.
2. **Strong:** flow texture, thermal bridge, frequency regime.
3. **Plausible but model-dependent:** collective phase lock, topological
   protection, parallel channels, interface support.
4. **Not evidence of superconductivity:** any RCCM narrative resemblance by
   itself.
