# TauLab Impact Assessment

## Scientific benefits

- Converts an evolving explanatory corpus into explicit falsifiable objects.
- Preserves failed models and negative results instead of rewriting history.
- Makes dimensional errors, target leakage, and boundary dependence
  mechanically visible.
- Creates a shared laboratory for comparing continuum hypotheses with
  established equations and experiments.
- Provides exact state datasets that may later support safe learned
  acceleration and game-scale reduced models.

## Scientific risks

| Risk | Consequence | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| A compelling simulation is mistaken for physical evidence. | Visual plausibility outruns validation. | Evidence ladder and claim-level certification. |
| Known physics is inserted into the tau solver. | Agreement becomes tautological. | Reference layer remains external; dependency audit. |
| Parameters are adjusted after every failure. | The model becomes unfalsifiable. | Immutable theory hashes and protected holdouts. |
| Discretization artifacts are named particles. | Grid defects masquerade as emergence. | Refinement, symmetry, and domain studies. |
| One successful benchmark is generalized to all physics. | Scope of evidence is overstated. | Per-claim validity domains and shared-parameter benchmark suites. |
| AI produces smooth but wrong trajectories. | Scientific output loses causal meaning. | Exact residual correction/replay, uncertainty, rollback. |
| Failed RCCM versions disappear. | Negative evidence and provenance are lost. | Append-only version and result storage. |

## Practical risks

- Full three-dimensional multiphysics fields are memory-bandwidth intensive.
- First-generation M1 hardware cannot resolve Planck-to-biological scale
  separation directly.
- Stiff relaxation and diffuse-interface terms may dominate timestep cost.
- GPU single-precision behavior may change event timing or topology.
- Checkpoint storage can grow faster than source code and must be
  content-addressed, chunked, and retention-aware.
- The theory formalization may reveal unresolved choices requiring new
  mathematical work before additional solver features are legitimate.

## Social and communication boundary

TauLab must not describe RCCM as validated merely because it has been made
executable. Public or internal reports must distinguish:

- “RCCM claims”;
- “TauLab RCCM-vN assumes”;
- “the numerical solver produced”;
- “the result converged”;
- “the result matched calibration data”;
- “the result passed a holdout”;
- “another group reproduced the result.”

## Game boundary

The future god-player interface is prosocial exploratory tooling, but free
state manipulation changes the causal question. Sandbox discoveries can
generate hypotheses; controlled or closed-universe replay is required before
they become scientific results.

## Current impact judgment

Proceed with the bounded foundation because it increases clarity even if every
RCCM candidate fails. Do not proceed to broad physical claims, biological
machines, or authoritative AI rollouts until the corresponding evergreen
razors have executable evidence.
