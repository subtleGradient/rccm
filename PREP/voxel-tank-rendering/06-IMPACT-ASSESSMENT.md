# Rendering and learning audit

## Practical adversary

- **Occlusion**: a full tank hides the gap. Study 03 defaults to a middle slice and offers the full volume; all tanks have a front view, orbit and reset.
- **Colour overload**: cyan stays slip; rose stays twist or separately captioned force. Charge identity is yellow/milky blue. Capacity and depletion mappings are named independently rather than silently sharing an amount convention.
- **Direction disappearing**: arrowheads and axes preserve sign even when magnitude and colour remain unchanged.
- **Aggregation erasing the mechanism**: mean and RMS are shown together, and reciprocal weights are averaged independently.
- **A toy becoming a false solver**: each scene names its authored, frozen specimen; controls change snapshots, sampling or visual mapping. Motion is taught through source-derived instantaneous operators.
- **A single local matrix pretending to know topology**: reference geometry and aggregate identity remain separate overlays.
- **External resources failing**: Three.js, OrbitControls and Inter are vendored with their licences. The pages need only a static localhost server and a WebGL browser; there is no build step or runtime CDN request.
- **Small-screen crowding**: main visual and controls stack; native controls have labels, keyboard input, visible focus and numeric equivalents. Mobile navigation uses a disclosure.

## Perception and stability evidence

- Tests-first matrix, overlap and sampled-face invariants were written before `math.mjs`. Initial failure was the missing implementation, followed by a green run.
- Independent source review checked the matrix, analytic curl and divergence, sign reversals, and pressure forces against the source route.
- Gaussian swirl speed is bounded by .38 per source, giving q>=.4224 for the two-source overlap fixture without clamping.
- Exact force-sign fixtures: opposite signs attract; same signs repel; reversing both signs preserves pressure; mass wells attract; uniform pressure cancels.
- Resolution tests compare complete volume averages across the same fixed lattice at all three resolutions and retain RMS under opposite-mean cancellation.

## Human outcome

The user receives three short physical manipulations with prediction/reveal prompts. The visual design is intended for a spatial thinker: place the fluid and boundary, alter one feature, read the invariant, then inspect the matrix or operation.

There is no new evidence of learner mastery. Next probe: flip slip while holding its magnitude, then increase its magnitude. Ask which tensor entries, capacity marks and pressure-budget segments change.
