# Working model: a voxel is an instrument over a volume

RCCM supplies the physics. Hyperslice and Quads-PREP test the visual encoding and teaching design. The pre-read prior remains in `01-HYPOTHESIS.md` for provenance.

## What survived

One all-purpose image cannot make every operation equally visible. Three coordinated studies cover distinct jobs:

1. **Local state**: composite cage, capacity bubble, planar instrument. Switch renderer without changing q, e, b or the matrix.
2. **Resolution**: independently sample two frozen toroidal reference defects at 1, 8 or 64 cells each. Change the partition without changing the fine field. Preserve both mean matrix and variation.
3. **Between objects**: point-sample an authored phase-overlap field and compare opposed pressure-face samples. Change signs while preserving absolute speed scales; read attraction, repulsion, mass-well pressure gradients and uniform squeeze.

The HTML map is `examples/voxel-tank/index.html`. The selected starting renderer is a fixed sampling cage with separate scalar, vector, oriented-loop and activity marks. Bubble and planar renderers are alternate visual languages, while reference particle geometry is an independent overlay.

## What the evidence changed

- The idea's scale ladder requires a coarse-state contract, not merely lower mesh detail. A voxel can contain a defect, atom, person or fraction of a planet.
- A mean q cannot reconstruct the averaged tensor: mean(1/q) and 1/mean(q) differ.
- One local matrix cannot recover a circulation route or certify a toroidal hole. Reference topology must have separate provenance.
- A bare torus lacks handedness. A directed loop or axis arrow adds the missing sign.
- The GfX file has a §16.2 interaction atlas after the fifteen-section route summarized in AGENTS. Its Coulomb-attraction entry directly supplies the aligned-gap-slip lesson.
- Force requires a neighbourhood operation. Colour for pressure alone cannot encode both scalar pressure and net force.

## Narrowed choice

Default: composite instruments on a slice through a 3D tank, with a selected-voxel matrix and pressure-face inspector. Keep the full 3D view for spatial placement. Add continuous volumes or object skins after the channel meanings are stable.

## Acceptance

The matrix/sign and pressure-face fixtures pass executable checks. Resolution aggregation is checked against a fixed fine lattice. Browser interaction and responsive checks are recorded in `06-IMPACT-ASSESSMENT.md`. Learner understanding remains to be measured through the prediction prompts; building an instrument does not answer those prompts for the user.
