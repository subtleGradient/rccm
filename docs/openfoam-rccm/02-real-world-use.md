# Real people, real OpenFOAM jobs

[Atlas](README.md) · [Jobs and hyperslices](01-jobs-and-hyperslices.md)

Research snapshot and link-access date: **2026-09-09**. This is a representative, expandable evidence ledger, not a census of every private installation. It establishes specific work that named people reported doing. A dated paper establishes use during that study; it does not establish that the same team is running the software today.

The two examples in the initial question already have counterparts here: predicting the flow around a supplied shape, and searching for a shape that improves a specified outcome. Their outputs include forces, temperature histories, mixing patterns, pressure loss, failure scenarios, and shape changes.

## Read the evidence labels first

| Label | What the source establishes |
|---|---|
| **2026 activity** | A dated 2026 release, research report, or direct practitioner statement supports activity at that date. It is not a live installation audit; a preprint is identified separately. |
| **Recent work** | Named work reported in 2024–2025. Continued use on the access date has not been independently established. |
| **Historical work** | A specific older use case, still useful for understanding the job; no inference of current deployment. |
| **Offering** | A currently accessible first-party product or project page describes available services or software. This alone does not establish a named customer's deployment. |

Keep software provenance separate from these dates:

- **Foundation** identifies the `openfoam.org` / `OpenFOAM/OpenFOAM-dev` family relevant to the supplied checkout.
- **OpenCFD** identifies the `openfoam.com` family, often carrying year-month version numbers such as `v2206`.
- **Extension** identifies an additional solver, coupling, or maintained branch. The underlying family is stated only when the inspected source establishes it.
- **Unresolved** means the use is evidenced but the exact distribution/version was not established in this research pass.

Examples from one family establish feasibility in that implementation. They do not establish that another family contains the same module or accepts the same case files. The Foundation's [development description](https://cfd.direct/about/) and OpenCFD's [project description](https://www.openfoam.com/) identify their respective development lines.

## Hyperslice the evidence, then try to break the slices

The reproducible target here is **a described simulation workflow**, not a whole company or industry. One company can occupy several cells, and one project can combine stages from both halves of a slice.

| Razor | Half A / half B | Placed cases | Counterexample or boundary exposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Which object is supplied?** | Shape fixed / shape varied by a search | Fixed: U02, U10, U13. Search: U01, U04. | An “optimized ship” paper can use OpenFOAM only for final forward checking. Record the component that performs the search separately. |
| **Where does the material move?** | Around a boundary / through a bounded apparatus | Around: U01, U04, U05. Through: U03, U07, U17. | A burning battery vents from an internal compartment into external air. Cut the subdomain, not the entire project. |
| **What creates the forcing?** | Prescribed mechanical/thermal conditions / an additional field model computed in the workflow | Prescribed: U13, U17. Coupled: U08, U11, U16. | “Electromagnetics” spans imposed magnetic forces, solved field equations, and multiscale coupling. Their software requirements differ. |
| **Does the workflow evolve a resolved fluid interface?** | Interface absent or fixed / interface position or shape solved over time | Absent/fixed: U01, U13, U22's diffusion tests. Evolving: U07. | A bubble can deform or coalesce while remaining gas: moving an interface does not itself require phase change or mass transfer. |
| **Does the material undergo thermodynamic phase change?** | No phase-conversion model / melting, solidification, evaporation, or condensation modeled | No conversion required: U07's bubble/interface motion. Conversion: U12, U15. | Latent-heat/phase-conversion laws are separate from interface geometry. U22 also shows species transfer across a fixed interface, which need not vaporize the carrier liquid. |
| **Where does model knowledge come from?** | Constitutive/kinetic inputs supplied / their values inferred or calibrated | Supplied: material laws across the ledger. Calibrated explicitly: U09. | These halves can occur in one run. Conventional “chemistry simulation” commonly advances supplied reactions and parameters; it does not imply discovering every law from a universal tensor. |
| **What is the evidence product?** | Numerical result or numerical cross-check / comparison with physical measurements | Numerical cross-check: U02, U04. Measurement comparison: U06, U09, U13, U16. | A paper may contain both. Reproducing a calibration experiment is a weaker test than predicting withheld experiments. |
| **What is the deployment claim?** | Research artifact / documented routine or offered workflow | Research: most rows. Direct routine-use statement: U05. Current offering: U01. | “Published in 2025,” “public repository,” “industry collaborator,” and “production operation today” are four different observations. |

The inversions are useful: a deliberately restricted research solver can be better for isolating a mechanism than a broad production package; a failed or discrepant calculation can expose missing physics; an old, pinned implementation can be easier to reproduce than an unpinned development checkout. None of these properties alone determines physical truth.

## What this changes about the RCCM opportunity

**Several apparently futuristic application categories already have conventional-model implementations:** electromagnetic heating, coupled nuclear-reactor physics, battery electrothermal/fire analysis, crystal-growth processing, and cooling of superconducting magnets. The missing value cannot therefore be described simply as “OpenFOAM gains electromagnetism, chemistry, or superconductors.” The application and the mechanism must be named separately.

An RCCM implementation would instead need to identify a concrete capability that its equations add: for example, a new constitutive prediction, a new coupling between fields, a material response that existing models cannot predict, or a quantitatively distinct experiment. Then it needs the same operational envelope seen in the strong examples above: state variables, parameters, geometry, initial/boundary conditions, output definitions, and measurement comparisons. See the [jobs map](01-jobs-and-hyperslices.md) and [atlas implementation route](README.md).

## Coverage limits and next evidence targets

The ledger deliberately prioritizes named users, a particular problem, and a visible output. Its 24 examples cover external aerodynamics, inverse design, rocket nozzles and plumes, engines, ships, wind farms, urban heat, multiphase reactors, induction heating, batteries/fires, nuclear multiphysics, latent-heat storage, biomedical flow, avalanches, casting, crystals, superconducting-magnet cooling, power electronics, HVAC tools, industrial acoustics, sewer mass transfer, food drying, and subsurface biogeochemistry.

It does **not** yet establish specific current deployments for pharmaceuticals/industrial bioreactors or offshore wave-energy structures. Several other territories now have a concrete research or historical development example without evidence of ongoing customer operation. These are evidence-search gaps in this ledger, not findings that OpenFOAM lacks the capability. Conference agendas, training-customer lists, and generic vendor industry pages are leads; they were not promoted into completed-use claims. U20 is explicitly a dated, client-specific contractor claim and carries its narrower evidence limit.

For the next entry, capture: **named practitioner → dated source → physical question → input model → simulation variant/version → measured output → comparison/validation → decision actually informed**. That keeps an expanding “all the jobs” map from turning into a list of marketing nouns.
